Learn to count cards in 7 easy levels

Count21 teaches you blackjack from zero to counting a real six-deck shoe with the Hi-Lo system, which is the method most professional counters start with. Each level has a short lesson, a practice drill and a clear goal. You move on when you hit the goal.

🧩 How the system works

  1. Play perfectly with basic strategy. This cuts the casino's edge to about 0.5%.
  2. Track the cards: low cards (2–6) = +1, middle cards (7–9) = 0, high cards (10–A) = −1.
  3. Convert the running count to a true count: running count ÷ decks left.
  4. Bet more when the true count is high and bet the minimum when it's low.
